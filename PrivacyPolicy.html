2. When you visit this website

When you open a page, GitHub Pages and the systems used to deliver the website process basic connection information, such as your IP address, the requested page, the time of the request, and browser details. This is needed to send the page to your device and to maintain the security and reliability of the service.

We rely on our legitimate interest in making this website available and protecting it from misuse (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub operates the hosting infrastructure and may process connection data under its own privacy terms. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement at https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement.

GitHub manages operational logs for its hosting service. We do not receive a separate copy of routine hosting logs through this website. If we need to retain information about a specific security incident, we keep it only for as long as needed to investigate and address that incident or meet a legal obligation.

This website loads fonts and interface resources from Google Fonts, Google Material Symbols, Font Awesome (served by cdnjs) and Tailwind CDN. When your browser requests these assets, the providers may receive connection details such as your IP address and browser information. Their own privacy notices explain how they process this information.

3. When you contact us

If you email info@kutas.org or send an enquiry through a form on our website, we use the information you provide—such as your name, business email address, organisation and message—to respond and discuss your request.

If you ask us to take steps toward a possible contract, we process the information for those pre-contractual steps (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). For other business enquiries, we rely on our legitimate interest in handling correspondence and responding to you (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Sending an enquiry does not sign you up for marketing.

We use your message to respond and manage our business correspondence. Access is limited to team members who need it to handle the enquiry. If your message does not lead to a contract, we delete it when it is no longer needed, unless a legal duty or claim requires us to keep it.

Please include only information needed for your request. Do not send passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information through a general enquiry.

4. Cookies, fonts and other services

Your browser stores your language choice in local storage so we can remember it on later visits. This preference remains in your browser. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies on this website.

External links take you to third-party websites. Those operators process information under their own privacy notices when you visit their pages.

5. Who may receive data, and where it goes

To run this website and respond to enquiries, we may share relevant information with our hosting, email and IT-security providers and with professional advisers. We share only the information needed for the relevant task.

Some service providers may process technical information in countries outside the European Economic Area. Where personal data is transferred internationally, the transfer must comply with Chapter V GDPR.

6. How long we keep information

We delete enquiry messages when they are no longer needed to respond, unless we need to keep them for a legal duty or claim. We retain business and accounting records for the periods required by law. GitHub manages its own hosting logs under its service practices.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you may have the right to request access, correction, erasure, restriction of processing and, where applicable, portability of your personal data. You may object to processing based on our legitimate interests on grounds relating to your particular situation. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time for the future; withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out lawfully.

You may complain to a supervisory authority. To exercise your rights or ask us about your personal data, write to info@kutas.org.

8. Automated decisions, children and changes

The business information provided describes B2B as the primary model, with possible B2G/B2NGO engagements and potential future B2C products. Confirm the actual audience of this website and any product before stating whether it is directed to children. Confirm whether any solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects are made; disclose their logic, significance and consequences if they are.

We will update this notice when our data practices or the website change. Notice version: 24 November 2026.
